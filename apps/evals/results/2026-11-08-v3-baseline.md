## D24a, and why it is not a verdict on anchors

D24a (`anchor-check.ts`, written after the smoke run) flagged **120 of 1215 anchors (9.9%) on the 174 valid zooms**, against 3 of 47 on the smoke. 53 are shape flags and 69 are bindings flags. I read them (sample, not all) and the rules mix real errors with false positives:

- **Bindings, 44 of 69:** the label edits a value that is outside every block the zoom block covers (`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` 17, `qwen3-coder-next` 9, `minimax-m3` 7, `gpt-5.4-nano` 6): the plan and the label disagree about what the block stands for, whichever block is the anchor.
- **Bindings, 25 of 69:** the value is inside another block the zoom block covers, but not inside its anchor (`deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` 8, `claude-haiku-5.5` 5). For example "print Fizz when divisible by 3" anchored on the `text_print` while the 3 is in the `if` condition it also covers. Whether that anchor is wrong is a judgement, not a rule.
- **Shape, 53:** 23 are a statement zoom block anchored on a `logic_compare`, 9 on a `variables_get`, 8 on a `math_arithmetic`. Some are plausibly wrong (a comparison is a condition, not the operation). **Some are false positives:** "return answer" is a statement zoom block whose only matching Detail block is the value in a function's return slot (`calculator` 12 flags, `tic-tac-toe` 6), and the rule cannot know that.

So the smoke's "flags exactly the three wrong ones" does not hold on 1215 anchors, as I said it could not be trusted ("a fit, not a test"). D24a is a **candidate generator for the labels**, not a measure of correctness. Nothing here says how many of the 120 flagged anchors are wrong, nor how many of the 1095 unflagged are.

## Limits

- One sample per cell; the L and XL cells are 4 and 1 programs. `gpt-oss-20b` is the cell most affected by rate limits.
- Pictures of this run were not drawn. D24 (is the anchor the right block) is not measured: that is the labelling step (plan v3 section 8).
- `v2` and `v3` were run on different days under the same prompt text apart from `covers`; provider behaviour can differ between days (the 429s are an example).

## Outcome: the `gpt-oss-20b` gap (added afterwards)

The same model on both prompts in one session ([`2026-11-08-gptoss-v2-v3.md`](2026-11-08-gptoss-v2-v3.md), 80 runs, $0.219) shows no sign of a `v3` effect: `v2` 19 of 40 valid, `v3` 26 of 40, and pooled over both days 35 and 33 of 60. The model's own spread between days is larger than the spread between prompts. The table above is unchanged.
