## What I looked at, and saw

I opened five pictures (the Detail program; `claude-haiku-5.5`'s Semantic level; `gpt-5.4-nano`'s two levels; `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`'s Semantic level). I did not open the other seven models' pictures. The manifest's drawn colour sets are `claude-haiku-5.5`'s three colours for seven models, those three plus the grey for `qwen3-coder-next` and `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, and a different set only for `gpt-5.4-nano`. A matching set does not prove each block has the right colour, so this is a hint, not a check.

- **`claude-haiku-5.5`: the colours are right, and the colour sets of nine of ten models match its own.** "set count to 10" and "decrease count by 1" are the variable pink, "repeat while" the loop green, "print" the text teal, as in the Detail program. The Concept block is the variable pink (its first stack is a "set count" block, a limit already noted in `2026-11-08-colour-spike.md`).
- **`gpt-5.4-nano`'s anchors resolve and are wrong.** Its `covers` list one id each, and "print count" is drawn blue, "decrement count by 1" teal and "print Liftoff!" pink, which is the colour of a variable block, not a text one. Its Concept block is grey because its plan lists no stack that exists. This is the "colour as a false cue" risk of plan v2 section 12.2, seen once: D23 counts it as resolved, only D24 (is the anchor the right block) would catch it.
- **`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it` left two blocks without covers.** They are grey, which is the neutral fallback working as designed, and it reads as "unknown", not as a wrong category.

## Limits

- Ten runs on one small program. The weak models' behaviour on larger programs is not shown; `countdown` has one stack and 14 blocks.
- Most models list covers in traversal order (`["b1","b2"]`, outermost first), so "the most telling block first" is mostly decided by the program's shape, not by the model weighing it. I did not test whether the sentence in the prompt changes that; it would need `v3` against a `v3` without the sentence.
- Nothing here measures D24. The proportion of right anchors is read off five pictures.
- The `v2` comparison (same program, same models) is not made here: the stored `v2` runs used earlier validators, and this is one sample.
