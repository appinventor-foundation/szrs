# `v2` and `v3` side by side, as the plugin would draw them: 2026-11-08

Nothing here is a new model run. Both prompts' zooms are stored: `v3` from [`2026-11-08-v3-baseline.json`](2026-11-08-v3-baseline.json), `v2` from [`2026-11-08-v2-baseline-2.json`](2026-11-08-v2-baseline-2.json). They are drawn with `--colour`: block definitions with no colour get the default grey, and the plugin colours each zoom block by its anchor in the run's stored plan, from a rendered Detail. A `v2` run has no `covers`, so its Semantic blocks are **grey by construction** and its Concept blocks are coloured from the plan's `stacks`; a `v3` run colours both. Made with `pnpm baseline:screenshots --results results/2026-11-08-v3-baseline.json,results/2026-11-08-v2-baseline-2.json --prompts v2,v3 --colour --name v2-v3-colour` (five programs, five models, the defaults of 2026-11-07; 24 pairs, as `qwen3-coder-next` has no valid Bank account run in either). Pictures: [`2026-11-08-v2-v3-colour`](2026-11-08-v2-v3-colour/); the gallery is `pnpm report results/2026-11-08-v2-v3-colour.json`.

**The two prompts are separate samples.** A difference in one picture's structure is mostly chance, not the prompt.

Of the 166 (program, model) pairs valid under both, 73 have the same number of Semantic blocks, `v3` has fewer in 52 and more in 41 (median difference 0), and the Concept count differs in 21. **`covers` does not visibly change the shape of the zoom.**

## What I looked at, and saw

I opened seven pictures (Bank account's Detail, and `claude-haiku-5.5`'s `v2` and `v3` Semantic and `v3` Concept; `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`'s `v3` Semantic on Bank account; `glm-5.3-flash`'s `v3` and `v2` Semantic on Tic-tac-toe). A sample, not a review.

- **`claude-haiku-5.5`, Bank account:** the `v3` Semantic level has the Detail program's colours: the procedure definitions and the deposit and withdraw calls purple, the `if` blue, `set balance` pink, `print` teal. The `v2` level of the same program is all grey. The colour is a real cue here: a reader sees which blocks are definitions and which are calls.
- **Its Concept level:** "deposit and withdraw money, refusing withdrawals that exceed the balance" is the procedure purple; "run a session that starts the balance at 100" is the variable pink, because the first stack of its concept is the `set balance` block. The colour says what the first stack is, not what the concept is (known limit, plan v2 section 4.3).
- **A wrong cue, `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, Bank account:** "deposit 50" and "withdraw 500" are drawn in the variable pink although they stand for the calls, which are purple in Detail. The same kind of error as `gpt-5.4-nano`'s in the smoke run, and a reader would take them for variable blocks.
- **`glm-5.3-flash`, Tic-tac-toe:** mostly plausible (variable pink for the setters, green for counting empty cells), but the whole "if X has completed a winning line, announce X wins; otherwise, if O…" chain is drawn in the text teal, so by its colour it was anchored on a `print`, not on the `if` it describes. I did not open that run's `covers` or the program's Detail, so I cannot say which block the anchor is or what the right colour for "check every winning line" (drawn `if` blue) would be. The `v2` version of the program is grey and says the same things in other words.

## Reading

`v3` gives the zoom the colour cue it did not have, and strong models use it well; it costs about 8% more output and 16% more money, and does not change the shape or the number of blocks. The cost of the cue is that weaker models sometimes give a block the wrong category's colour, which is worse than grey because it looks like information. How often is what the labels (plan v3 section 8) are for.

## Limits

- Seven pictures out of about 100 drawn (48 runs, two levels each, and the five Detail programs); different samples for `v2` and `v3`.
- `v2` Semantic grey is by construction, not a finding.
- The plugin's colour step is the same in both columns; only the plan differs.
